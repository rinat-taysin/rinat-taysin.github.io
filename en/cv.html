RISK — technical director / lead developer

Aug 2026 – present, part-time

A closed B2B platform, vrisk.ru, for carriers and freight forwarders who weren’t paid for a trip. The client is TEK Antares, Kazan. Case study

  • From the case documents, AI (Claude Sonnet and Haiku) writes the pre-trial claim and the court filing, which is submitted through “Moi Arbitr”. Code calculates the penalty as a table for the judge, and the platform calls the debtor on its own.
  • The first version took 7 weeks and went live on vrisk.ru on 21 Sep 2026, with 16 releases in the 3 weeks since. One developer with Claude agents: ~980 commits, 187 ADRs. The client’s lawyer and its head are piloting the platform, and staff are joining.
  • The AI adapter hides personal data: before a model call, names, phone numbers, emails and passport details are swapped for placeholders, and restored after the reply. The call log is stored without texts.
  • A model reply is accepted only if every amount, date, number and legal article in it appears in the input. Otherwise the document gets the template text.
  • AI features are switched on by flags as the lawyer checks them, and the product also works without a model. A claim draft is ready in about 45 seconds. The assistant answers only from knowledge base cards (pgvector search).

Node 22, TypeScript, Hono, Drizzle ORM, PostgreSQL + pgvector, Redis, S3; React, Vite, Ant Design; Docker Compose, GitHub Actions; Claude Sonnet and Haiku
